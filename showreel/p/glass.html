Window Glass Planner

Fake stained glass from fluted film

Overview

Plans a stained-glass look for two windows using clear and tea-tinted reeded film, then works out how to cut it all from two sheets.

  • Cut pieces from what is left of each sheet; drag them onto the windows
  • Flute direction respected, pieces rotate and re-pack automatically
  • Print-ready spec: window overlay, cutting sheets and a full cut list
