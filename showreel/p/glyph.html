Glyph Sky

Weather & planes on a phone's LED back

Overview

An Android app for the Nothing Phone's round LED matrix. Face down, it shows the temperature and an animated weather icon; when a plane flies within 5 km it flies across the lights.

  • 12 animated weather icons plus UV index on a 137-LED circle
  • Planes cross in their real heading, then scroll flight, route, type, airline and speed
  • Privacy-minded: precise location stays on the phone, only a rounded point is sent
  • Installed and updated straight from GitHub releases via Obtainium
Built withKotlin · Android · Nothing Glyph SDK · adsb.lol · Open-Meteo
