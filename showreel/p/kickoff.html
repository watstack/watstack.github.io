Kickoff

World Cup 2026 sweepstake & betting

Overview

A pixel-art sweepstake for the 2026 World Cup. Create a tournament, share one link, run a live team draw, then follow the bracket together.

  • Live animated draw that hands every mate their teams
  • Virtual-coin betting: match result, correct score, double chance, to qualify and goalscorer markets
  • Odds refreshed on a schedule by GitHub Actions; bets settle automatically after full time
  • Push notifications, installable as an app
  • A Claude-written "betting bulletin" roasting the group every 3 days in WhatsApp
Built withVanilla JS · Supabase · Vercel functions · GitHub Actions · PWA
