Decorator

2D floor plan & furniture planner

Overview

Our house traced from its floor plan to the millimetre, so furniture can be dropped in to scale before anything gets bought.

  • Every labelled room dimension honoured; walls draggable to correct the trace
  • Furniture library (sofas, tables, beds) placed by plan or wall elevation
  • Doors, sliders and windows drawn with real opening heights
  • Snap to grid and walls, original plan as a ghost layer behind
Built withVanilla JS · SVG · unit-tested model
